1. 25-Point Diagnostic Inspections

Mint & Mop conducts comprehensive 25-point physical and thermal imaging assessments of residential and light-commercial attics and crawl spaces. Inspections include digital moisture metering, FLIR thermal imaging for heat/cold bypasses, visual inspection of insulation integrity, structural framing checks, and rodent entry corridor mapping.

Initial diagnostic inspections are 100% complimentary with zero obligation. You will receive a written digital photo inspection dossier detailing all findings prior to any contracted work.

2. Upfront Pricing & Estimate Guarantee

All price quotes issued following an on-site diagnostic inspection are fixed and guaranteed for 30 calendar days. We guarantee zero hidden travel fees, disposal surcharges, or post-job surprise fees. Any optional scope adjustments requested by the client during active work will require an executed digital change-order before commencement.

3. OSHA Containment & Isolation Protocol

To guarantee that no rodent feces, airborne fungal spores, or insulation particulate contaminate your living quarters, all remediation operations are executed under OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1101 negative-pressure safety standards:

  • Airlock Isolation: Living room, hallway, and attic access openings are isolated with magnetic zipper containment chambers.
  • Negative-Air HEPA Scrubbing: Commercial HEPA extraction units operate continuously, venting vacuumed air directly to the home’s exterior.
  • Protective Floor Sheeting: All hallways and interior paths are covered with tear-resistant adhesive poly sheeting.

4. Lifetime Rodent-Free Seal Warranty

All structural rodent exclusion work completed by Mint & Mop—including 1/4-inch heavy-gauge galvanized hardware cloth reinforcement, metal flashing, and expanding foam chimney seals—is backed by our Lifetime Rodent-Free Seal Guarantee.

Transferable Warranty: If rodents chew through or breach any sealed exclusion point at any time while you own the home (or transfer to a new buyer upon written notification within 30 days of home sale), Mint & Mop will re-seal and re-sanitize the affected attic space at zero cost.

5. Botanical Sanitization Standards

Our ULV cold fogging treatments exclusively utilize EPA List N registered botanical disinfectants (active ingredient: pure natural Thymol). These hospital-grade formulations kill 99.99% of bacteria, fungal spores, hantavirus, and salmonella on contact. They are non-toxic, non-corrosive, safe for children and household pets, and leave zero synthetic chemical residues.

6. Residential Payment Terms

We believe in earning homeowner trust through verifiable results:

No Upfront Deposit

For all residential services under $2,500, no deposit is required prior to project start.

Pay Upon Inspection Approval

Payment is due upon completion after you inspect the work and review your digital before-and-after photo clearance report.

7. Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy

Homeowners may reschedule or cancel any scheduled diagnostic inspection or non-emergency remediation project at zero cost up to 24 hours prior to the designated arrival window. For emergency Sunday or holiday decontamination, cancellations within 4 hours of arrival may be subject to a nominal dispatch fee.

Mint & Mop Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 2026 • CCPA & GDPR Compliant
1. Information We Collect

When you request an inspection or remediation estimate, Mint & Mop collects your name, property address, contact phone, email, and diagnostic findings including thermal infrared imaging scan photos, moisture meter percentage readouts, and attic/crawl space condition photos.

2. How Inspection Data & Photos Are Used

Diagnostic photos and sensor data are collected solely to generate your itemized 25-point inspection report, calculate upfront remediation pricing, and maintain our lifetime seal warranty database. We never sell, rent, or lease your personal information or property photos to any third-party marketing firms.

3. Digital Photo Storage & Encryption

All property assessment records, FLIR thermal photos, and before/after verification photos are stored in secure AES-256 encrypted cloud storage accessible only by your certified remediation crew and project supervisor.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You retain the right to request access to your full inspection file, obtain copies of your digital diagnostic reports, or request complete removal of your property records from our cloud database by contacting our Data Protection Officer.
