1. Information We Collect

Mint & Mop ("Mint & Mop", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting homeowner privacy and transparency. When you schedule a complimentary 25-point attic or crawl space inspection, request emergency remediation, or use our digital customer portal, we collect specific property and contact details necessary to perform decontamination safely and effectively.

  • Personal Identifiers: First and last name, residential property address, primary contact telephone number, and email address.
  • Property Architecture Details: Approximate square footage, foundation type (pier-and-beam, crawl space, slab-on-grade), roof pitch, accessibility points, and HVAC ductwork configuration.
  • Diagnostic Findings: Environmental moisture readings, ambient attic relative humidity, infrared thermal thermal differentials, and airborne mold particle counts.

2. Diagnostic Photos & Sensor Telemetry

To provide full photographic transparency, our technicians capture high-resolution before-and-after photographs and FLIR thermal imaging scans of damaged insulation, rodent intrusion corridors, compromised vapor barriers, and fungal contamination.

Homeowner Guarantee: Photographic records are strictly confined to unfinished attic cavities, crawl spaces, and exterior venting perimeters. Technicians are legally prohibited from capturing any inhabited living spaces without explicit prior written homeowner consent.

3. Cloud Encryption & Data Security

Every photographic inspection dossier, moisture telemetry log, and clearance certificate is transmitted via TLS 1.3 encrypted protocols and securely archived using AES-256 cloud encryption. Access is restricted through multi-factor authentication (MFA) exclusively granted to your assigned field project supervisor and licensed remediation specialists.

4. Non-Disclosure & Zero Third-Party Sale Pledge

We maintain a strict zero-compromise policy regarding homeowner information:

Never Sold or Rented

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your contact details or inspection results to data aggregators, marketers, or third parties.

Strict Need-To-Know

Information is accessed strictly for estimating, permitting, scheduling, and warranty claim tracking.

5. Lifetime Seal Warranty Verification

To honor our transferable Lifetime Rodent-Free Seal Warranty, we securely maintain warranty records linked to your property parcel ID. Should you sell your home, the prospective homebuyer can verify warranty coverage seamlessly through our customer service registry without disclosing private prior homeowner financial records.

6. Your Rights Under CCPA & GDPR

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have complete legal rights regarding your personal records:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete export of all diagnostic photos, thermal reports, and service invoices stored in your customer file.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your customer account and non-regulatory records at any time.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercising your data privacy rights will never result in altered pricing or denial of emergency decontamination services.

7. Contact Our Data Protection Officer

For formal data access requests, deletion requests, or questions regarding our environmental data retention policies, please reach out to our dedicated privacy compliance team:

Mint & Mop Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 2026 • CCPA & GDPR Compliant
1. Information We Collect

When you request an inspection or remediation estimate, Mint & Mop collects your name, property address, contact phone, email, and diagnostic findings including thermal infrared imaging scan photos, moisture meter percentage readouts, and attic/crawl space condition photos.

2. How Inspection Data & Photos Are Used

Diagnostic photos and sensor data are collected solely to generate your itemized 25-point inspection report, calculate upfront remediation pricing, and maintain our lifetime seal warranty database. We never sell, rent, or lease your personal information or property photos to any third-party marketing firms.

3. Digital Photo Storage & Encryption

All property assessment records, FLIR thermal photos, and before/after verification photos are stored in secure AES-256 encrypted cloud storage accessible only by your certified remediation crew and project supervisor.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You retain the right to request access to your full inspection file, obtain copies of your digital diagnostic reports, or request complete removal of your property records from our cloud database by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

Mint & Mop Terms of Service
Standard Customer Service Agreement • Updated October 2026
1. Diagnostic Inspection & Scope of Work

Our 25-point attic and crawl space inspection is 100% complimentary with zero booking obligation. Upfront written quotes remain guaranteed for 30 calendar days from the date of initial diagnostic inspection.

2. Safety Containment & Negative Pressure Protocol

Remediation crews operate strictly under OSHA 29 CFR containment regulations. Living quarters are isolated with magnetic zipper airlocks, and negative air HEPA machines are vented to the exterior to guarantee zero indoor cross-contamination.

3. Lifetime Rodent Seal & Workmanship Warranty

All exclusion repairs executed with 1/4" galvanized steel mesh and fire-rated expanding foam carry a transferable Lifetime Rodent-Free Seal Warranty. Botanical antimicrobial treatments carry an unconditional 1-year odor and mold neutralization guarantee.

4. Payment & Cancellation Policy

Residential services do not require upfront deposits for jobs under $2,500. Payment is due upon completion and review of your digital photo clearance report. Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 24 hours prior to service arrival.
