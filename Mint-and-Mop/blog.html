Mint & Mop Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 2026 • CCPA & GDPR Compliant
1. Information We Collect

When you request an inspection or remediation estimate, Mint & Mop collects your name, property address, contact phone, email, and diagnostic findings including thermal infrared imaging scan photos, moisture meter percentage readouts, and attic/crawl space condition photos.

2. How Inspection Data & Photos Are Used

Diagnostic photos and sensor data are collected solely to generate your itemized 25-point inspection report, calculate upfront remediation pricing, and maintain our lifetime seal warranty database. We never sell, rent, or lease your personal information or property photos to any third-party marketing firms.

3. Digital Photo Storage & Encryption

All property assessment records, FLIR thermal photos, and before/after verification photos are stored in secure AES-256 encrypted cloud storage accessible only by your certified remediation crew and project supervisor.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You retain the right to request access to your full inspection file, obtain copies of your digital diagnostic reports, or request complete removal of your property records from our cloud database by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

Mint & Mop Terms of Service
Standard Customer Service Agreement • Updated October 2026
1. Diagnostic Inspection & Scope of Work

Our 25-point attic and crawl space inspection is 100% complimentary with zero booking obligation. Upfront written quotes remain guaranteed for 30 calendar days from the date of initial diagnostic inspection.

2. Safety Containment & Negative Pressure Protocol

Remediation crews operate strictly under OSHA 29 CFR containment regulations. Living quarters are isolated with magnetic zipper airlocks, and negative air HEPA machines are vented to the exterior to guarantee zero indoor cross-contamination.

3. Lifetime Rodent Seal & Workmanship Warranty

All exclusion repairs executed with 1/4" galvanized steel mesh and fire-rated expanding foam carry a transferable Lifetime Rodent-Free Seal Warranty. Botanical antimicrobial treatments carry an unconditional 1-year odor and mold neutralization guarantee.

4. Payment & Cancellation Policy

Residential services do not require upfront deposits for jobs under $2,500. Payment is due upon completion and review of your digital photo clearance report. Rescheduling requests must be submitted at least 24 hours prior to service arrival.
